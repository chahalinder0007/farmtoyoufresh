# What the 4.16% on our turmeric means

*October 2026*

Before our turmeric goes on sale, we send a sample to a lab. Our first batch, L-01, came back at **4.16% curcumin**. Here's what that number means, and what it doesn't.

## What curcumin is

Curcumin is the natural compound that gives turmeric its deep yellow colour. Turmeric contains three closely related compounds, together called curcuminoids, and curcumin is the main one.

## How a lab measures it

Labs usually use one of two methods:

- **HPLC** separates curcumin from the other two curcuminoids and measures each one.
- **UV spectrophotometry** measures all the curcuminoids together, and usually reads a little higher.

A result can also be given "as is" or on a "dry basis", after allowing for moisture, which changes the number slightly. That's why we always say which batch a number belongs to.

## How 4.16% compares

India's food regulator, FSSAI, requires turmeric powder to contain at least **2.0% curcuminoids** on a dry basis. Batch L-01 is about double that minimum. Turmeric varieties differ a lot, and some, like Lakadong from Meghalaya, are sold with much higher figures. We'd rather give you a real, tested number than a big one.
